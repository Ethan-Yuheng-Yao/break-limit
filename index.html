Face your enemy to block. A fresh guard can parry. Use your shield (2 / 9): Kairo blocks and repels; Vex absorbs damage for a stored burst. Skills automatically aim at your opponent. Direct attack impacts freeze both fighters before a slow-motion shield clash; an equal or stronger shield throws the attacker down. A higher-form attacker breaks your defense and knocks you down, but the blocked attack deals no damage. Attacking builds ultimate energy; taking hits builds it faster. Both players have cooldown panels in their bottom corners. Tap your first skill repeatedly to chain three fast attacks; the third hit knocks down or launches. These attacks can be interrupted, have a 0.35-second cooldown and 0.1 seconds of recovery. Other skills have 0.4 seconds of recovery. Distant attacks are absorbed without flinging their caster. Against an airborne skill, the later slam or ultimate redirects its own power upward to intercept: lightning splits meteors, gravity catches them, and singularities swallow incoming attacks. Equal or stronger defenses stop the attack; the attacker backflips to their original position. Higher-phase attacks break through at 40% damage. Movement dashes travel farther with each evolution; awakened Kairo becomes lightning and awakened Vex becomes a void rift. Stunned and knocked-down fighters can still be displaced by hits. Thunderfall and Crush cannot be interrupted or knocked back during their animation, but still take damage. Restrained or knocked-down fighters cannot act until released.

Movement combos: hold jump for height, or release early for a short hop. Jump over creature bites and low ground blasts; tall lightning and airborne attacks can still hit you. Dash in midair, then cast a skill. After your first skill connects, chain into a jump, dash, or another ready skill. Other damaging skills can chain after their animation ends; look for CHAIN READY. Cooldowns and ultimate energy still apply. Press slightly early during recovery to queue your next action. Armored slams and ultimates always finish their animation.

Campaign: clear nine levels with three solo bosses. Boss 1 upgrades your phase; defeating Boss 2 awakens you. Each boss is the campaign character, CPU controlled at your phase. Cleared levels save as checkpoints; defeat retries the current level. Choose a campaign, then enter with a fighter you already own. Clearing the campaign unlocks its character. All creatures match the campaign’s theme. Regular enemies have overhead health bars; bosses have a top health bar. Tab changes your campaign target. Kairo and Vex start unlocked. Nyra’s trial is Expert difficulty; Ronin’s is Master.

First to 3 wins. After a round the winner heals 40% of missing health; the loser evolves, gains maximum health, and fully heals. Maximum HP per tier: 200 / 260 / 320 / 400. When your rival reaches 2 points, you awaken. At 2–2, enter the Fractured Heavens. Esc pauses. Reduced FX softens flashes and disables camera shake.
