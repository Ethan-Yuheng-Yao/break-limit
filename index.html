Face your enemy to block. A fresh guard can parry. Use your shield (2 / 9): Kairo blocks and repels; Vex absorbs damage for a stored burst. Skills automatically aim at your opponent. Shield impacts freeze both fighters before a slow-motion clash; an equal or stronger shield throws the attacker down. A higher-form attacker breaks your defense and knocks you down, but the blocked attack deals no damage. Attacking builds ultimate energy; taking hits builds it faster. Both players have cooldown panels in their bottom corners. Each skill has 0.4 seconds of recovery after its animation. Thunderfall and Crush cannot be interrupted or knocked back during their animation, but still take damage. Restrained or knocked-down fighters cannot act until released.

First to 3 wins. After a round the winner heals 40% of missing health; the loser evolves, gains maximum health, and fully heals. Maximum HP per tier: 200 / 260 / 320 / 400. When your rival reaches 2 points, you awaken. At 2–2, enter the Fractured Heavens. Esc pauses. Reduced FX softens flashes and disables camera shake.
